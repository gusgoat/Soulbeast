Data use

Contact: Line @soulbeast. We use your email account, account identifier, characters and progress for authentication, saving, ownership checks and coupon delivery. Order identifiers, payment references and refund states support fulfillment, duplicate prevention and purchase support. Providers may process connection, device and diagnostic information for their services. The game does not store full card numbers, CVCs or banking passwords.

Request account and character deletion through the deletion page or settings. Pending purchases need review first. Some transaction and refund records may be retained for dispute resolution and the seller's obligations. Contact support for retention details or data requests.
