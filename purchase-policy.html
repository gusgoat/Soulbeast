Purchases and refunds

Coupons are digital game items with no cash redemption or withdrawal. Purchases are one-time, with the price shown before confirmation. On the web, pay by Thai QR and upload your receipt for manual review. Android payments use Google Play. Choose the recipient before purchasing. Each discounted pack bought by QR is limited to once per character and is checked against that character’s existing orders. Google Play follows the terms shown in the app. Review the current gacha probabilities and guarantees before buying.

Coupons are delivered after bank payment review or payment-provider confirmation. Check an existing pending order before paying again. For delivery or refund help, contact Line @soulbeast with your order ID. Google Play purchases also follow Google's refund process. Refunds and disputes adjust the coupon entitlement; partial refunds round the proportional coupon reversal up. Spent coupons may create a review balance that pauses further purchases. A provider-confirmed won dispute restores the remaining entitlement.

购买与退款

兑换券是游戏数字物品，不可兑换现金或提现。每次购买均为一次性付款，确认前显示价格。网页通过泰国二维码付款，上传付款凭证后等待人工核对。Android 通过 Google Play 付款。购买前请选择接收角色。使用二维码购买优惠礼包时，每个角色每种礼包限购一次，系统也会核对该角色已有的订单。Google Play 以应用内显示的规则为准。购买前请查看最新扭蛋概率与保底规则。

管理员核实银行入账或支付服务商确认付款后发放兑换券。再次付款前请检查待处理订单。发放或退款问题请携订单号联系Line @soulbeast。Google Play交易亦按其退款流程处理。退款或争议将调整兑换券权益，部分退款按比例向上取整扣回。已使用的兑换券可能产生待审核余额并暂停后续购买；支付方确认争议胜诉后恢复剩余权益。
