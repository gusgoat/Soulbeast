Purchases and refunds

Coupons are digital game items with no cash redemption or withdrawal. Purchases are one-time, with the price shown before confirmation. Web payments use Stripe; Android payments use Google Play. Choose the recipient before purchasing. Each first-purchase pack is limited to once per email account across both channels. Review the current gacha probabilities and guarantees before buying.

Coupons are delivered only after verified payment. Check an existing pending order before paying again. For delivery or refund help, contact Line @soulbeast with your order ID. Google Play purchases also follow Google's refund process. Refunds and disputes adjust the coupon entitlement; partial refunds round the proportional coupon reversal up. Spent coupons may create a review balance that pauses further purchases. A provider-confirmed won dispute restores the remaining entitlement.

购买与退款

兑换券是游戏数字物品，不可兑换现金或提现。每次购买均为一次性付款，确认前显示价格。网页通过Stripe付款，Android通过Google Play付款。购买前请选择接收角色。每个首购礼包在同一邮箱账户中仅可使用一次，网页与应用共享资格。购买前请查看最新扭蛋概率与保底规则。
