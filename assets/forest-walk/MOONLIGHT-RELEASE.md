# Moonlight forest release · 2026-11-19

User-approved publication of zone 1 walking scenes, layered PNG/Blender scenery, original native event IDs, full-screen menus and same-place returns. Includes idle-traveler depth ordering, bounded fire/water effects, sealed boss chambers and separate boss guardian statues. Training shares those effects and its approved boss doorway presentation.

Scene action buttons, including opening chests, sit in a right-hand group above the footer ornament. The positioning wrapper prevents the menu's later generic button styles from returning them to document flow. Verify real hit targets, footer clearance and original chest rewards on portrait and landscape viewports in tools/test-forest-actions-browser.mjs.

Only zones 1 and 7 have walking scenes. The prepared doorway designs for other zones do not enable their walking systems. Desert walking is subsequent local preview work and is excluded from this release.

Media: 21 moonlight atlases/renders, 8 door kits and 8 guardian-pair atlases, 37 PNG files total. tools/publish-forest-media.mjs prepares a manifest, uploads new content-addressed objects without replacement and verifies public bytes with SHA-256. Local credential input stays outside the repository. Generated images remain ignored.

Validation: native event/ordinary-map browser coverage, same-place returns, duplicate-reward guards, original secret and boss battles, depth-order and fire/water pixel regressions, transparent source bounds, open doorway approach, mobile menu layout, and public asset/release smoke checks.
