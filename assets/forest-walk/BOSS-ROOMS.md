# Closed boss chambers

Eight themed door kits are prepared for every native area. Training (zone 7) and moonlight (zone 1) currently use them in walking scenes. Mist (zone 2), desert (zone 3), dragon (zone 4), fallen heaven (zone 5), demon castle (zone 6) and binding forest (zone 8) are design assets for future walking scenes; this does not enable those scenes. Original boss species, levels, menu tokens, combat and rewards remain owned by the native game.

Zone 8 is named ป่าทมิฬพันธนาการ in the native menu and country entrance catalogue (not ป่าทมิฬบรรพกาล). It unlocks after the demon lord, uses levels 55–60 and has the original Ferroth/Mortivar duo boss at level 60. Check the complete ForestEntranceData.zones catalogue as well as late ZN.push additions; the initial ZN factory alone omits later areas. The unit check compares all design zone IDs against that native catalogue.

Open tools/boss-room-preview/index.html through the local preview server to switch between all eight assembled rooms or inspect their six separate PNG cutouts. Gallery placement uses the shared bossRoomParts geometry. Only the two existing walking areas have playable preview links.

Generated with built-in imagegen. Transparent PNG atlases are in ignored generated/boss-rooms/. Each theme contains six independent cutouts: stairs, rear facade, left wall, right wall, arch crown and closed double door. Source rectangles are recorded by tools/inspect-boss-room-alpha.py without changing image pixels.

Additional guardian statues are separate transparent atlases in generated/boss-statues/. Each assembled theme now includes eight visible cutouts: the six original architectural parts plus left/right boss statues. See BOSS-STATUES.md for reference identities and generation prompts.

The existing boss approach contact remains at 4.4 world units. Door and facade collisions leave this approach reachable. The boss room uses a terminal path and minimap instead of Y branches. Boss actors are shown by the original boss menu/battle rather than standing outside the closed door.

No production publication or Storage upload is included in this preview.
