# Sleeping Dragon Peak local PNG assets

Seventeen PNGs generated with built-in imagegen are saved in ignored generated/dragon/: fifteen modular environment/place atlases, one six-hazard atlas, and one opaque tileable rocky-ground texture. No new images have been uploaded or added to GitHub for this local preview.

woodland.png received an imagegen transparency extraction, preserving the six objects and camera while removing its white studio backdrop. The original draft remains in D:/GameDev/soulbeast/tmp/dragon-source-drafts/. tools/inspect-dragon-alpha.py reads source rectangles without changing pixels, including complete pine silhouettes across their nominal cell boundary. All final cutout atlases have genuine alpha-zero backgrounds and no neighbouring-piece strips.
