## Blender architecture update

The visible walls, columns, lintels and door surrounds now use real evaluated Blender mesh geometry instead of the original architecture PNG cutouts. Source: `generated/demon-architecture/demon-castle-architecture.blend`; rebuild: `tools/build-demon-castle-architecture.py`; runtime mesh export: `generated/demon-architecture/meshes.js` (929,060 bytes). The seven modular meshes include ashlar stone, recessed mortar, bevelled edges, cornices and open arch apertures. Side walls are physically rotated in Blender, so they expose the correct thickness and top surfaces.

`castle-architecture.js` projects those faces through the game's fixed orthographic camera and caches the resulting transparent surfaces. This preserves the existing 2.5D character compositor and foreground fading without requiring a second WebGL renderer. Short fitted wall spans scale mesh X before projection, preserving wall height; lintels meet column capitals in the same projection. Furniture, torches, floor, boss door and statues retain their existing art. Original tree scenery remains in steps 1–3. Native event contacts, Y exits, wall/column collision positions and combat rules are unchanged. `tools/test-castle-architecture.mjs` checks thickness, orientation, open apertures, fitted height and collision placement.

## Progression and native behavior

Steps 1–3 retain the existing outdoor deadwood and PNG props. From step 4 onward, each Y map is a furnished interior room. The native depth counter records completed encounters, so a pending map at depth 3 is the fourth step. `walk.castleStep` and `walk.castleRoom` persist the visual decision while its event resolves, preventing scenery from changing after a menu or battle. The existing native Y-exit planner still owns route/event RNG. Room labels are derived from that same saved prediction without drawing extra randomness or revealing hidden destinations.

The room types are ห้องใต้ดิน, คุก, ห้องแสดงดนตรี, ห้องอาหาร, ห้องนั่งเล่น, คลังอาวุธ, ห้องทดลองเวทมนตร์, ห้องประกอบพิธีกรรม, ห้องสมุด, ห้องจัดงานเลี้ยง, ห้องสมบัติ, plus the boss's ท้องพระโรง. The types follow the original events and ordinary locations; they do not add extra steps to fit every room into one run. The demo picker can show every type directly.

Native secret, special, riddle, tale, quest, cost, probability, reward, inventory and combat data are unchanged. Original well contact geometry, service entrances and same-place menu returns remain. Native event data are compared with production `7c4bd95c` by `tools/test-demon-rooms.mjs`.
